Even Odds Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2026

Even Odds does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connections. Your display settings are saved only on your device, and the dice rolls the app generates exist only on your device while the app is running.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
